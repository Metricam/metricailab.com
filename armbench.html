Open Benchmark Suite

ArmBench

The open benchmark suite for Armenian language AI, built and maintained by Metric AI Lab. Two live leaderboards: ArmBench-LLM for large language models and ArmBench-TextEmbed for text embedding models.

ArmBench-LLM

ArmBench-LLM evaluates large language models on Armenian-language tasks — measuring how well frontier and open models actually understand and generate Armenian. It is the first open LLM benchmark for the Armenian language, and the leaderboard below is live: it updates as new models are evaluated.

ArmBench-TextEmbed

ArmBench-TextEmbed benchmarks text embedding models on Armenian retrieval and semantic similarity tasks. Our own ATE-1 and ATE-2 Armenian text embedding models rank #1 — outperforming Gemini and OpenAI embeddings on every Armenian task.
