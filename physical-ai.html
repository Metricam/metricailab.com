What we build

Three problems we solve.

Each one starts as a scoped pilot on your data — robots, cameras, or both.

For robot builders

Robotic Failure Detection

Every undetected failure burns margin and trust. We built FailBench — a pooled benchmark for robotic arm failure detection — and FailDetect, a model that zooms, crops, and catches failures. We deploy the same stack on your fleet logs.

Fleet-scale failure detection pilots
VLM & VLA fine-tuning for your tasks
Evaluation harnesses that predict field performance
For video-rich operations

Industrial Video Intelligence

Inspection drones, site cameras, production lines — imagery piles up faster than analysts can review it. We fine-tune VLMs that turn raw footage into defect reports and alerts, then distill them to run at the edge.

Automated inspection reporting with custom VLMs
Defect, corrosion & anomaly detection models
Distillation for real-time edge inference
For outdoor autonomy

Perception in Unstructured Environments

Fields, orchards, and construction sites don't look like training data. We build perception that survives occlusion, lighting, and seasonal drift — and the data engine to keep it accurate.

Crop, weed & obstacle discrimination
In-field failure detection — no operator nearby
Seasonal retraining pipelines & data engines
